AI Proof Guest Arrival Checklist October 2026 Complete

Hosting guests at your private pool property should feel seamless, safe, and enjoyable for everyone. This checklist, generated using Founders AI Generation Proof, walks you through every step from the moment guests arrive to when they leave. Follow it to reduce confusion, protect your property, and create a five‑star experience.

Directions and Access

Clear address and landmarks

  • Provide the exact street address, unit number (if applicable), and any gate codes.
  • Mention nearby landmarks (e.g., “next to the blue mailbox”, “across from Oak Grove Park”) to help guests spot the property quickly.
  • Include latitude/longitude coordinates for GPS devices.

Pre‑arrival communication

  • Send a digital map link (Google Maps or Apple Maps) 24 hours before check‑in.
  • Highlight any tolls, construction zones, or one‑way streets guests should avoid.
  • Note the best time of day to arrive to bypass rush‑hour traffic.

Access instructions

  • Detail how to unlock the main gate or door (keypad code, lockbox, smart lock).
  • If a physical key is required, specify where it’s hidden and how to return it.
  • Offer a backup contact number in case of access issues.

Parking

Designated spots

  • Mark the number of guest parking spaces available and any resident‑only zones.
  • Indicate whether parking is covered, uncovered, or valet‑provided.
  • Note any height restrictions for RVs or trailers.

Rules and etiquette

  • Ask guests not to block driveways, fire lanes, or neighbor’s spaces.
  • Remind them to keep music low and engines off while parked.
  • Provide a short list of items prohibited in the parking area (e.g., open flames, chemicals).

Guest convenience

  • Place a small sign or cone to reserve spots if needed.
  • Offer a towel rack or shoe mat near the parking area for pool‑ready guests.
  • Ensure lighting is adequate for night arrivals.

Check‑in

Welcome packet

  • Leave a printed welcome card with Wi‑Fi name/password, emergency numbers, and checkout time.
  • Include a brief property tour guide (where towels, pool chemicals, and extra blankets are stored).
  • Add a QR code linking to a digital version of this checklist.

Verification steps

  • Confirm the number of guests matches the reservation.
  • Take a quick photo of the pool area for your records (optional, with guest consent).
  • Verify that any requested amenities (e.g., floaties, BBQ grill) are set up and functional.

Personal touch

  • Greet guests by name if you meet them in person.
  • Offer a chilled bottle of water or a local snack as a welcome gesture.
  • Ask if they have any immediate questions or concerns.

Safety Orientation

Pool rules

  • Post clear signage: no diving, no glass containers, children must be supervised.
  • State the pool depth markers and any slip‑zone warnings.
  • Remind guests to shower before entering the pool to maintain water quality.

Emergency equipment

  • Show where the life ring, first‑aid kit, and fire extinguisher are located.
  • Explain how to shut off the pool pump in case of an electrical issue.
  • Provide the address of the nearest hospital and the non‑emergency police line.

Hazard awareness

  • Identify any trip hazards (e.g., pool deck cracks, loose tiles) and mark them with tape or signs.
  • Note if the pool cover is manual or automatic and how to operate it safely.
  • Advise guests to keep pets away from the pool unless they are strong swimmers and supervised.

Amenities

Poolside essentials

  • Confirm the availability of lounge chairs, umbrellas, and side tables.
  • Check that pool toys, noodles, and volleyball nets are clean and ready.
  • Ensure the grill or outdoor kitchen is stocked with propane/charcoal and utensils.

Indoor comforts

  • Verify linens are fresh, towels are plentiful, and extra blankets are in the closet.
  • Test the HVAC system and provide remote control instructions.
  • Stock basic toiletries (soap, shampoo, toilet paper) and note any refill schedule.

Entertainment options

  • List streaming services available on the smart TV and provide login instructions if needed.
  • Offer board games, books, or a Bluetooth speaker for poolside music.
  • Mention any nearby attractions (hiking trails, restaurants) with distance and travel time.

Checkout

Final walk‑through

  • Ask guests to do a quick visual check for forgotten items (phones, chargers, swimwear).
  • Inspect the pool area for debris, trash, or equipment left out of place.
  • Confirm that all windows are locked, lights are off, and the thermostat is set to an energy‑saving level.

Key return

  • Instruct guests on how to lock up and return keys/access devices.
  • Provide a drop‑off location for lockbox keys or a smart lock deactivation code.
  • Thank them for staying and invite feedback via a short survey link.

Post‑departure actions

  • Schedule a pool cleaning and chemical balance check within 24 hours.
  • Wash and fold used linens, replenish consumables, and restock the welcome basket.
  • Update your calendar noting any maintenance issues reported by guests.
